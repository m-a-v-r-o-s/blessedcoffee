Privacy Policy

Blessed Coffee & Spirits · Last updated 26 October 2026

Who we are

Blessed Coffee & Spirits operates this website and two coffee shops:

  • Blessed Coffee 2024, Rodou 68, Athens 104 45, Greece. Phone 211 218 1815 or 693 291 7800.
  • Blessed Coffee Kamatero, Kosti Palama 17A, Kamatero 134 51, Greece. Phone 210 238 8812.

What information we collect

This website does not ask you for any personal information. There are no forms and no accounts. It stores two small preferences in your browser's local storage, which never leave your device:

  • Shop. The shop you picked (Patisia or Kamatero), so the order and call buttons point to that shop on your next visit.
  • Nothing else. We do not run analytics or advertising tracking. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Cookies and third-party content

The site sets no cookies of its own. Its fonts are served from our own server. Two things only contact other companies when you choose to use them:

  • Google Maps. Each shop page has a map that loads only when you press "Show map". Google may then set cookies and receives your IP address, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Delivery and social links. Links to e-food, Wolt, Box, Instagram, Facebook and Google reviews take you to those sites, which have their own policies.

Job applications

If you apply for a job by phone, in a shop or on Instagram, we use what you tell us only to consider your application and contact you about it, and we delete it when the hiring process is over. We do not sell or share it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by calling either shop. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
